# CPAMP Mobile 1.4.10

本版本集中改进账号页面在小屏手机上的信息密度与扫读体验，并将账号详情重构为更紧凑的扁平分区布局。

## 中文

### 📊 账号列表

- 针对 6.3 英寸手机优化账号页面边距、筛选栏、健康概览和内容密度。
- 将每个账号改为独立卡片，通过醒目的供应商标识、账号名称、套餐和状态胶囊提高辨识度。
- 将主要额度周期移到账号信息下方，以全宽进度条同时展示周期、剩余比例和风险状态。
- 改进停用账号的呈现，明确说明停用期间不会展示当前额度。

### 🧾 账号详情

- 采用“详情信息优先”布局，以紧凑身份卡和连续扁平分区组织账号信息。
- 收紧小屏页面边距，并简化额度窗口、重置时间和剩余比例的视觉层次。
- 将当前周期的请求、成本、Token 和成功率整理为稳定的两列指标网格。
- 优化预计成本、Codex 重置额度和数据来源分区，同时保留原有刷新、确认和兑换流程。

## English

This release improves account-page density and scanability on compact phones and reorganizes account details into a flatter, more focused layout.

### 📊 Account list

- Optimized account-page spacing, provider filters, health summaries, and content density for 6.3-inch phones.
- Presented each account in a separate card with a prominent provider mark, account identity, plan, and status badge.
- Moved primary quota windows below account identity and expanded progress rows to show periods, remaining percentages, and risk states clearly.
- Improved disabled-account presentation with an explicit explanation that current quota is hidden while the account is disabled.

### 🧾 Account details

- Adopted a detail-first layout built around a compact identity card and continuous flat sections.
- Tightened small-screen margins and simplified the hierarchy of quota windows, reset times, and remaining percentages.
- Organized current-cycle requests, cost, tokens, and success rate into a stable two-column metric grid.
- Refined projected cost, Codex reset credits, and data-source sections while preserving existing refresh, confirmation, and redemption flows.
