# CPAMP Mobile 1.4.6

本版本进一步优化移动端底部导航的液态玻璃质感，使静止状态更加清澈，并让切换与按压反馈更自然。

## 中文

### ✨ 液态玻璃导航

- 缩窄浮动底部导航并采用完整胶囊轮廓，让导航在手机屏幕上更轻盈、聚焦。
- 为选中项加入弹性跟随、按压收缩和短暂动态折射，使切换过程更连贯。
- 降低玻璃表面的主题色覆盖、模糊和阴影强度，改善透明度与背景层次。
- 将彩色像差限制在交互过程中，避免静止状态出现持续彩边。
- 保留旧版 Android 的高不透明度回退，确保导航内容清晰可读。

## English

This release further refines the liquid glass bottom navigation on mobile, with a clearer resting appearance and more natural selection and press feedback.

### ✨ Liquid glass navigation

- Narrowed the floating bottom navigation and adopted a full capsule silhouette for a lighter, more focused mobile layout.
- Added elastic selection tracking, press compression, and brief dynamic refraction for smoother transitions.
- Reduced surface tint, blur, and shadow intensity to improve transparency and background depth.
- Limited chromatic aberration to active interactions so the resting glass remains clean.
- Preserved the high-opacity fallback on older Android versions for reliable readability.
